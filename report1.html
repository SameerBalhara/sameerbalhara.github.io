Deep Reinforcement Learning in AWS DeepRacer

PPO, curriculum training, reward shaping, and transfer learning across autonomous driving tasks.

Added May 15, 2026Report

Overview

This report explores how Deep Reinforcement Learning (Proximal Policy Optimization) can train an AWS DeepRacer agent across increasingly difficult autonomous driving tasks. Beginning with time-trial completion, the project extends into obstacle avoidance and head-to-bot racing through staged curriculum training, reward shaping, and transfer learning. The final policy achieved 100% lap progress across all benchmark tracks for five consecutive evaluation episodes in each phase.
